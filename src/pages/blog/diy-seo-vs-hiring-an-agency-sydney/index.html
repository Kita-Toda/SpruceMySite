DIY SEO Or Hire An Agency? Here's The Honest Answer.

It's not a question of which one 'works' — both can. It's a question of what you're actually trading, and whether that trade suits where your business is right now.

C Christian Alba, SpruceMySite · 8 September 2026 · 2 min read

Every SEO agency's blog post about DIY SEO says the same thing: don't do it yourself, it's too risky, hire us. We're not going to do that, because it's not honest. DIY SEO genuinely works for plenty of small businesses. The real question isn't "which one is better" — it's what you're trading either way.

What SEO Actually Involves

Stripped of jargon, ongoing local SEO usually means: keeping your Google Business Profile accurate and active, building and maintaining consistent citations (your business listed correctly across directories), earning and responding to reviews, publishing content that answers real customer questions, fixing technical issues that quietly hurt rankings, and tracking whether any of it is actually moving the needle.

The Real Cost Of DIY

It's rarely a cash cost — most of the tools have free tiers. The real cost is time, and more specifically, consistent time. SEO rewards businesses that keep showing up over months, not the ones that do a big push in January and go quiet by March. If you're already stretched thin running the actual business, that consistency is the hardest part to sustain — not the knowledge.

What Sydney Agencies Typically Charge, And Why It Varies

Pricing swings a lot based on competitiveness of your suburb and industry, how much content and technical work is needed, and whether it's bundled with web design or maintenance. Rather than chasing the cheapest number, ask what's actually included for that price — some retainers cover strategy and reporting only, with no actual content or technical work happening underneath it.

When DIY Genuinely Makes Sense

If you're pre-revenue or very early, have more time than budget, and you actually enjoy this kind of work — go for it. Google Business Profile optimisation and review generation alone will get a new local business further than most people expect.

When It Doesn't

When your time is worth more spent on client work, when six months of DIY effort hasn't moved rankings and you don't know why, or when you're in a genuinely competitive suburb where every competitor already has professional SEO running in the background. At that point, the "savings" of DIY are really just deferred cost.
