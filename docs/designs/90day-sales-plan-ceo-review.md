## Vision

### 10x Check
The 10x version of this plan is not "more outreach" — it is a repeatable sales engine
where every asset compounds: a defined productized offer (package + price floor) that
every conversation sells, one CRM (GoHighLevel) holding every lead — warm, cold, AND
inbound — visual-proof cold outreach (annotated screenshots/Loom of the prospect's own
site), and a referral expectation seeded at kickoff so each delivered customer
mechanically produces the next 2-3 leads. Customer #1 stops being a milestone and
becomes the ignition of a flywheel. Effort delta over the current plan: S or S-M per
item (expansion #1 is M); the heavier production work is sequenced after Day 14 so the
pre-close window stays protected.

### Day 21 gate — branch criteria (expansion #4)
- **Contract signed by Day 21** → gate passes; continue plan as written.
- **No contract but a proposal is live** (delivered, awaiting decision) → gate passes
  conditionally; set a 72-hour decision deadline with the prospect; re-check Day 24.
- **No proposal live with any warm lead** → promote the cold track: 50 sends/week,
  annotated screenshots on the top 15 by score, plain template sends for the rest,
  time-boxed at 2 hours/day; demote warm follow-ups to 30 min/day. Day 45 gate remains
  the hard fallback for a full strategy pivot.
