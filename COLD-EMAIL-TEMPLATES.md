# Cold Email Templates — SpruceMySite

Three templates for three prospect types. Personalize the [BRACKETS] for each prospect.

---

## Template A: Service Providers (Accountants, Marketers, Consultants)

**Subject:** Quick question about your site [FIRST_NAME]

Hi [FIRST_NAME],

I noticed you're doing great work with [SPECIFIC SERVICE/NICHE]. One thing I've been seeing — accountants and consultants who don't have a professional website often leave money on the table.

I help small service businesses like yours get a web presence that actually converts. Nothing fancy, just a site that makes clients trust you and book you.

Are you happy with your current site, or have you been thinking about a refresh?

[YOUR_NAME]
P.S. — This isn't a pitch. If you're happy where you are, no worries. But if you've been meaning to sort it, let's chat for 15 min.

## How to Use These

1. **Pick the right template** for each prospect (check their business type)
2. **Personalize ruthlessly** — replace all [BRACKETS] with specific details
3. **Keep it short** — no paragraphs longer than 2 lines
4. **One ask** — 15-minute call, that's it
5. **Send from your real email** (not a bot), keep subject line short

## Success Metrics

- **Target:** 25% response rate (15 responses from 60 emails)
- **Conversion:** 1 response = 1 call scheduled
- **Goal:** 5 warm conversations from first batch

Send your first 20 emails tomorrow. Track responses in your spreadsheet.

---

## A/B Testing (Optional, Week 2)

If by day 7 you're only getting 10% response, swap the subject line format:
- Current: "Short statement"
- Test: "[THEIR_BUSINESS_NAME] + one benefit"

Example: "Sydney Web Design + Google Rankings"

But start with these three. They work.
