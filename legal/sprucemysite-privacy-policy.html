Our position under the Privacy Act

The Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) currently applies in full to organisations above a turnover threshold, and to some smaller organisations regardless of turnover. Rather than argue about whether we sit inside or outside that threshold on any given day, we have chosen to handle personal information in accordance with the 13 Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) as a matter of policy, and we commit to the practices set out below.

03What We Collect

Information you give us

  • Contact and identity details — name, business name, role, email address, phone number, postal or billing address.
  • Project information — your website URL, existing accounts and logins you choose to share, brand assets, content, and anything you send us to build with.
  • Payment information — billing details and transaction records. We do not store full card numbers; card data is handled directly by our payment processor.
  • Correspondence — emails, messages, call notes, and meeting records.

Information we collect automatically

  • Technical data — IP address, approximate location derived from it, browser and device type, operating system, referring page, and pages viewed.
  • Usage data — how you move through our site, what you click, and how long you stay, collected through cookies and analytics (see §8).

Information we collect from other sources

We build lists of local businesses we think we can help, using publicly available sources. That research may capture personal information about the people who run those businesses.

  • Public business listings — Google Business Profile, business websites, social media profiles, and public directories. This can include a named owner or manager, a business email address, and a phone number.
  • Public reviews — we read published customer reviews of a venue to understand its service issues. We record the substance, not reviewer identities.
  • Referrals — where an existing client passes on your details, we will tell you who referred you when we first make contact.

Where we collect information about you from somewhere other than you, we will tell you we hold it, and where we got it, at or around the time we first contact you. If you would rather we didn't hold it, say so and we will delete it — see §11.

04Why We Collect It

  • To quote for, deliver, support and improve our web design, development and automation services.
  • To issue invoices, take payment, and chase overdue accounts.
  • To communicate with you about a project, respond to enquiries, and provide support.
  • To contact local businesses about services we believe are relevant to them (see §7).
  • To understand how our website performs so we can improve it.
  • To meet legal and tax obligations, and to establish or defend a legal claim.

We will not use your personal information for a purpose unrelated to the one we collected it for, unless you'd reasonably expect it or you've agreed.

05Dealing With Us Anonymously

You can browse our website without telling us who you are, and you can ask a general question by phone without giving your name. We can't deliver a project, issue an invoice, or run a site audit anonymously — those need real details.

07Marketing And Outreach

We send commercial electronic messages — email and SMS — to businesses we think we can help, and to people who have asked to hear from us.

  • Every message identifies us and includes a working unsubscribe option.
  • Unsubscribes are honoured within five business days, and usually immediately.
  • Where we contact a business using an address published for that business, we rely on the message being relevant to that person's role. If it isn't relevant, or you'd simply rather not hear from us, reply and we'll stop.
  • Opting out of marketing does not stop service messages — invoices, project updates, and security notices still come through while you're a client.

When we run campaigns on behalf of a client, that client is responsible for holding the necessary consent for their own contact list.

08Cookies And Analytics

Our website uses cookies — small files stored on your device. We use them to keep the site working, remember your preferences, and measure traffic.

  • Essential cookies keep the site functioning. These can't be turned off.
  • Analytics cookies tell us which pages get read and where visitors come from.
  • Advertising cookies, where used, let us measure whether our ads work.

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Some parts of the site may stop working properly if you do.

10Security

We protect personal information with measures appropriate to a business of our size: encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), reputable hosting and cloud providers, access limited to people who need it, multi-factor authentication on business accounts, and password management rather than shared credentials.

No system is perfectly secure, and no transmission over the internet is guaranteed private. We can't promise absolute security — only that we take it seriously and act quickly when something goes wrong.

If there's a data breach

If personal information we hold is lost or accessed without authorisation, and that is likely to result in serious harm, we will assess it promptly and notify the affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner in line with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

11Your Rights

Access

You can ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy. We'll respond within 30 days. It's free, though we may charge a reasonable cost for a large or complex request — we'll quote it before doing the work. We may need to verify who you are first.

Correction

If something we hold is wrong, out of date or incomplete, tell us and we'll fix it. If we disagree that it's wrong, we'll tell you why and you can ask us to attach a statement noting your view.

Deletion

You can ask us to delete personal information we hold about you. We'll do it unless we're legally required to keep it — tax records being the usual reason.

Opting out

You can unsubscribe from marketing at any time using the link in any message, or by emailing us.

12Information Sent Overseas
